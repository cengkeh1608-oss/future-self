One funny text message from your future self, under 200 characters. Entry for the
[Texts From Your Future Self](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e)
competition on make.fun.

> "Me from 2031. The novel you were going to write? I finished it. It's four pages. Page three is just the word 'the'."

`main.ts` writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`.
